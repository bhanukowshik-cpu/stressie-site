02Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. Please read our privacy policy to understand how we handle your information.

  • Authentication: We utilize Firebase as an authentication service to secure your account and ensure a seamless and safe login experience.
  • Data Usage: All data collected within Stressie is anonymized through Firebase to protect your identity and personal information. We adhere to HIPAA guidelines and regulations in the handling of identifiable information. The resulting anonymized data is used to provide anonymous profile insights from registered users to inform wellness programs. No identifiable information is shared with Stressie, except for our trusted technical team who has access to Firebase's program for maintenance purposes. Additionally, no identifiable information is shared with the registered user's company.
  • Personalized Engagement: The data we collect is solely used to personalize your experience within Stressie. This includes tailoring content, features, and recommendations to suit your preferences and needs.

By using Stressie, you consent to the terms outlined in this privacy policy.

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring a secure and enjoyable experience within Stressie. If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please don't hesitate to contact us.

03Getting help

In a crisis, call or text 988 in the US.Free and confidential, 24 hours a day.

Stressie does not diagnose mental health conditions. If you or someone you know is suffering, please consider seeking help from a licensed mental health professional or doctor. For immediate assistance, you can contact the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline at 1-800-273-TALK (1-800-273-8255) or visit their website at www.suicidepreventionlifeline.org. Additionally, if you or someone you know is struggling with addiction, you can contact the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) National Helpline at 1-800-662-HELP (1-800-662-4357) or visit their website at www.samhsa.gov.
