How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Step 1 of 3

  1. Share one company code. Your team joins in minutes, on their own phones.

    Built for shift and frontline work, not desk schedules.

  2. What people do in Stressie is private. You never see names, only how the team is doing as a whole.

    Nothing shows until there's enough data to protect everyone.

  3. Get anonymous trends and simple next steps, like a ten-minute team Huddle.
