Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1st, 2025

Stressie (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile app (“the App”) and our analytics dashboard (“the Dashboard”). By using Stressie, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

01Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • App Users: Individuals who use the Stressie app to engage with wellness and stress management tools.
  • Dashboard Users: Authorized representatives of client organizations who access aggregated wellness engagement data via the Stressie Dashboard.

02What Information We Collect

a. App Users

We collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Email address or other login credentials (for account creation and access).
  • Engagement Data: Interactions with features like coping tools, affirmations, quick resets, and earned Coping Points.
  • Wellness Preferences: Optional data provided through onboarding and usage to personalize your experience.

Note: All data used for Dashboard reporting is aggregated and anonymized. We do not share individual user activity or identifiable information with employers or third parties.

b. Dashboard Users

We collect:

  • Login Credentials: To securely authenticate access to the Dashboard.
  • Usage Logs: Time of access and features viewed, for internal analytics and support.

03How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and personalize your Stressie experience.
  • Track engagement trends across the workforce (in aggregated form).
  • Improve product features and overall platform effectiveness.
  • Support customer service and technical needs.

04Data Aggregation and Anonymity

All data shared through the Dashboard is:

  • Aggregated: Combined across users to show trends, not individual behaviors.
  • Anonymized: Stripped of personally identifiable information.

Employers and Dashboard users cannot view or access individual user data or identity-linked wellness activity.

05Data Security

We take data security seriously. Our safeguards include:

  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular audits and monitoring for vulnerabilities

Your personal information is stored securely, and we use best practices to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

06Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party tools for hosting, analytics, or push notifications. These vendors are contractually bound to maintain confidentiality and data protection standards that align with this policy.

07Your Rights and Choices

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Correct or update your information
  • Delete your account or data at any time (via settings or by contacting us)

For deletion requests or data inquiries, please contact info@stressieapp.com

08Children’s Privacy

Stressie is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

09Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we will notify you via the app or email. Continued use of the platform after such updates constitutes agreement to the revised policy.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact:
