How it works

From listed to booked in three steps.

Step 1 of 3

  1. Tell us about your massage, class, coffee or event, when you're open, and how many points it costs.

    We help you set it up.

  2. People close to you see your offer in Discover, on the map, when they're looking for a reset.

    With a Stressie Partner badge.

  3. They show the pass in their Wallet at your front desk. Scan it, and they're in.

    No new hardware needed.
